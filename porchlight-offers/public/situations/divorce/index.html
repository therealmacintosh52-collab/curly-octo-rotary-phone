FAQ

Divorce: common questions

Do both spouses have to sign?

Anyone on title must sign, and in community property and homestead states a non-title spouse often must sign too. Your attorney can confirm what your decree requires.

Can you close before the divorce is final?

Often yes, if both parties agree to the sale terms in writing. Many couples prefer to convert the house to cash first so there is a number to divide instead of an asset to fight over.
