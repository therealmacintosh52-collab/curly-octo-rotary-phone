How we handle it

  • You pick the date

    Close before you leave, or after — whatever fits the move. We work backward from your date.

  • Rent-back if you need it

    Need to stay a couple weeks after closing? Ask. A short post-closing occupancy is usually workable.

  • Sign remotely

    Remote online notarization and mobile notaries mean you can close from the new city. Funds are wired.

  • No holding costs

    The mortgage, taxes, insurance, utilities and lawn care stop the day we close instead of six months into a listing.

FAQ

Relocating: common questions

Can you close in under two weeks?

Frequently, yes. Cash purchases skip the appraisal and underwriting, so the schedule is set by title work rather than a lender.

What if my company offers a relocation buyout?

Compare both. A corporate buyout program may net you more — take the higher number. We will happily be the backup offer.
