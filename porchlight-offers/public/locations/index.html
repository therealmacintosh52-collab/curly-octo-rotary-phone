Where we buy houses in Dallas–Fort Worth

We are local: local buyers, local title companies, local contractors. Pick your city below, or call (555) 012-3456 — if you are anywhere in Dallas–Fort Worth, we probably buy there.

  • We buy houses in Dallas

    Dallas County

    Dallas has two housing markets running side by side: renovated homes that sell in a weekend, and everything else. If your house needs a roof, foundation work, or a full interior update, it competes against move-in ready listings — and buyers with FHA financing often cannot close on it at all.

  • We buy houses in Fort Worth

    Tarrant County

    A lot of Fort Worth housing stock is pre-1970, and older homes come with older problems: cast iron sewer lines, aluminum wiring, original HVAC. Those are the exact items that blow up a traditional sale at inspection.

    76104 · 76105 · 76110 · 76112

  • We buy houses in Arlington

    Tarrant County

    Arlington is a landlord-heavy market, and we buy a lot of tired rentals here — properties with deferred maintenance, mid-lease tenants, or a turnover bill the owner does not want to pay. Sell it occupied, as-is, and let us handle the rest.

    76001 · 76010 · 76011 · 76013

  • We buy houses in Plano

    Collin County

    Plano homes sell — but the 1970s and 1980s original-condition houses in central Plano sell at a discount and only after months of showings. If you are relocating for work and cannot carry two mortgages, a cash close on your date is usually worth more than the extra list price.

    75023 · 75074 · 75075 · 75023

  • We buy houses in Garland

    Dallas County

    Garland is full of solid mid-century homes owned by the same family for decades. When those houses transfer — usually through an estate — they need everything: kitchen, baths, systems, roof.

    75040 · 75041 · 75042 · 75043

  • We buy houses in Irving

    Dallas County

    Irving sits on top of the DFW Airport job corridor, which means a lot of sudden moves. If a transfer, a job loss, or a family change means you need to be out fast, you should not have to spend $15,000 getting a house ready for strangers to walk through.

    75038 · 75060 · 75061 · 75062

  • We buy houses in Mesquite

    Dallas County

    Mesquite is an affordable, owner-occupied market where a failed inspection can kill a deal outright — most buyers here are financed and their lender will not fund a house with an active roof leak or a dead HVAC. A cash buyer removes the lender from the equation entirely.

    75149 · 75150 · 75180 · 75181

  • We buy houses in Grand Prairie

    Dallas & Tarrant Counties

    Grand Prairie straddles two counties, which means two tax offices, two sets of records, and title work that can get messy — especially on inherited property or houses with old liens. We buy through a local title company that untangles it.

    75050 · 75051 · 75052 · 75054
