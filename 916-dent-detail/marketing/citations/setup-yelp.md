# Yelp for Business: Setup (as of 2026-10-15)

Status: DRAFT. Owner executes (creating accounts is a hard gate). Sources: no Yelp-official page surfaced in search. Steps are from BrightLocal https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/how-to-add-or-claim-a-yelp-business-listing/ and Uberall https://uberall.helpjuice.com/locations/631331 (retrieved 2026-10-15; third-party). Screens may differ: **verify in dashboard**.

Time: ~25 min plus verification.

## Before you start
- Have the canonical NAP (`nap-format.md`), logo, 5+ real photos, hours, service list.
- Decision D2: whether to show an address. Reported: the address step can be skipped for service-area businesses and Yelp supports a hidden address (third-party). Do not enter a fake address.

## Steps
1. Search Yelp for "916 Dent & Detail" and for the phone number. If a page already exists (even unclaimed), claim it. Do not create a duplicate. If a duplicate already exists, use "suggest an edit" to flag it, then contact Yelp support to merge (anecdotal, https://localsearchforum.com/threads/yelp-is-displaying-the-wrong-business-page.62440/).
2. Go to https://biz.yelp.com and choose to add or claim a business. Create or sign in to a Yelp account with a business email.
3. Enter business name exactly `916 Dent & Detail`, phone [OWNER PHONE], website https://[OWNER DOMAIN]/.
4. Address: choose the option for a business that serves customers at their location / does not have a public address, if offered. If Yelp insists on an address, stop and ask the owner (D2).
5. Choose categories: search "Auto Detailing" and "Windshield/dent" style categories; Yelp's category list is its own: **verify in dashboard** which dent/PDR category exists. Pick only true ones.
6. Service area: add the cities from `gbp/categories.md` if the form allows.
7. Verify (phone call or code, per Yelp).
8. Fill Info: hours, specialties, history (use the GBP description, `gbp/description-750.md`), "from the business" text. No years in business or awards unless true. [OWNER]
9. Upload photos from `gbp/photo-shot-list.md` (real only).
10. Settings: turn off "Request a quote" only if you will not answer; otherwise route to a monitored inbox. 

## Things to decline
- Yelp Ads and "Yelp for Business" paid upsell calls. No payment info entered without owner approval (hard gate).
- Review solicitation: Yelp discourages asking for reviews (Yelp's own guidance, common knowledge, UNVERIFIED today). Do not run the GBP review campaign on Yelp; at most put "find us on Yelp" without asking for a review. Verify Yelp's current policy before adding any Yelp link in review messages.

## Done check
Name, phone and website match NAP. Photos in. Duplicate none. Log in tracker.
