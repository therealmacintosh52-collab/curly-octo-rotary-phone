## Steps
1. You must be an owner, employee or authorized representative (Nextdoor Business guide).
2. Go to https://business.nextdoor.com/local and choose to sign up or claim a free Business Page. Or in the app: profile photo > "Add Business Page" if you already have a neighbor account.
3. Search by business name and location. If the page exists, claim it; if not, "Create your business page".
4. Enter name `916 Dent & Detail`, phone [OWNER PHONE], website, email, hours, and category (Nextdoor's own list: verify).
5. Verification may be a phone call to the business number (Nextdoor Business guide). Casual services may need documents.
6. Service area: no dedicated setting was found in search. Ask in the app which neighborhoods your page can reach. Business posts reportedly let you choose the audience radius or neighborhoods (third-party). **Verify in dashboard.**
7. Add logo, a greeting that tells the story, and photos (real only).

## Rules to respect
- Nextdoor is neighborly, not a billboard. One useful post a week, reply to every comment. 
- Recommendations from neighbors are organic. Do not ask friends to fake recommendations. Do not offer rewards for recommendations (same ethics as Google reviews).
- Do not buy Nextdoor ads without a budget decision (D4 in README).
