# Review Reply Templates

Status: DRAFT. Owner posts replies (hard gate: publishing). Reply within 48 hours (best practice, UNVERIFIED source). Public replies: never reveal customer details, price, address or private information.

Voice: plain, warm, short. Never argue. Never offer money for a changed review (incentives for changing reviews are a policy violation; see review-requests.md sources).

## 5-star
```
Thank you, [FIRST NAME]. We are glad the [SERVICE] turned out the way you wanted. If you need anything else, send a photo anytime. - [OWNER NAME], 916 Dent & Detail
```
Variant for dent work:
```
Thank you, [FIRST NAME]. Good to hear the dent is gone and your paint is untouched. Enjoy the car.
```
Variant for coating:
```
Thank you, [FIRST NAME]. Hope the beading keeps making you smile on wash day.
```

## 3-4 star (mixed)
```
Thank you for the honest feedback, [FIRST NAME]. We are glad [WHAT WENT WELL], and we hear you on [THE ISSUE]. That is not the standard we want. Please call or text [OWNER PHONE] so we can make it right. - [OWNER NAME]
```
Rules: name one real positive, one real issue, no excuses, give a direct contact, then follow up privately.

## 1-2 star (real customer)
```
We are sorry the experience fell short, [FIRST NAME]. We would like to understand what happened and fix what we can. Please reach us at [OWNER PHONE] or [OWNER EMAIL] with the date of service. - [OWNER NAME]
```
Rules: do not dispute facts in public, do not reveal job details. After the private call, you may add a short update reply if the customer agrees. Do not ask the customer to change or delete the review in exchange for anything.

## Review for a job we cannot find in /admin (possible fake or wrong business)
```
Thank you for the feedback. We cannot find a record of a job under this name. Please contact us at [OWNER PHONE] with the date and vehicle so we can look into it.
```
Then flag it (below).

## Fake, spam, conflict of interest or off-topic
1. Do not reply with accusations.
2. In the Business Profile dashboard open Reviews > the review > Report review (or flag in Maps). Dashboard wording: verify in dashboard.
3. Reasons that fit: fake engagement, conflict of interest (competitor), off-topic, spam, impersonation. Google lists these as prohibited content (source: https://www.demandhub.co/articles/google-review-policy via search, 2026-10-15; third-party).
4. Keep a screenshot and the date. Re-check in a few days. Escalate through GBP support if still live.
5. Optional neutral public reply: the "can't find a record" template above.

## Competitor sniping or pressure
Do not retaliate with reviews of their business. Do not ask friends/family to flood reviews (fake engagement). Collect real reviews through the normal ask process.

## Never
- No promises you cannot keep ("we will refund everything").
- No reply with your own phone number if the owner prefers the business line: use [OWNER PHONE].
- No self-reviews, staff reviews, or reviews from the same device/location.
