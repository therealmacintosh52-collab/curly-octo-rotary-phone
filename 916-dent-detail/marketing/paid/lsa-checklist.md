All of this is UNVERIFIED. The sign-up flow tells you the truth for your ZIP: https://ads.google.com/local-services-ads/ (URL not opened today; verify).
- If neither category is available, LSA is not an option. Do not stretch the business description to fit a category.

2. Write down which categories it offers for auto services. Screenshot.
3. If dent removal is absent and detailing is offered, decide (D5) whether detailing-only LSA is worth it for a service that may not be the main profit center.

## Requirements to expect (general LSA practice, UNVERIFIED today; read Google's list in the flow)
- Business license and insurance proof where required by category; background checks for owners/technicians (varies by category).
- Pay-per-lead model with a weekly budget; you can dispute invalid leads inside LSA.
- Service area and hours must match the Business Profile (`gbp/`).
- Profile needs reviews eventually; a new profile may have none (do not buy or solicit unfairly; see `gbp/review-requests.md`).
- Photos and a short business description (reuse `gbp/description-750.md` trimmed to what the LSA form allows).
- Answering calls: LSA leads are calls and messages; missing them hurts ranking (general guidance).
