## C. Stormwater and mobile washing (precision flags)
- Core rule: only clean water to storm drains; car-wash/detailing wastewater is a prohibited discharge [SRC https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/content/dam/portal/dou/utilities/stormwater/stormwater-quality/documents/CommAutoWashing_English.pdf | 2026-10-15; search summary "discharging wash water, cleaning agents ... to the storm drain is illegal"]. That sheet targets commercial auto washing; applicability to a mobile operator is by inference [UNVERIFIED classification].
- Penalty: "up to $5000 per day" under County ordinance, city "similar" [SRC https://waterresources.saccounty.gov/rfcw/Documents/carwash_apr09_standard.pdf | 2026-10-15 summary]. Confirm in the ordinance text.
- West Sacramento: check flow; direct water to vegetation/gravel or sanitary sewer where possible [SRC cityofwestsacramento PDF above].
- Orange/San Diego counties require capture and sanitary-sewer disposal [SRC https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/dpw/watersheds/IndustrialCommercial/Mobile_Vehicle_Washing.html | 2026-10-15]; not Sacramento law, only a benchmark.
- Central San (Contra Costa, NOT Sacramento) requires a free mobile-washer permit and lists zero-discharge reclaim washers [SRC https://centralsan.org/sites/main/files/file-attachments/mobile_washer_fact_sheet.pdf | 2026-10-15]. Do not cite as Sacramento rule.
- Sacramento Regional County Sanitation District mobile-washer requirement: NOT FOUND [UNVERIFIED]. Free help: Business Environmental Resource Center (916) 649-0225 / sacberc.org (per search summary) - owner may call; I did not.
- Practical, hedged: waterless/low-water products and capture-and-reclaim avoid the question; never let rinse go to gutter; PDR work needs no water. Hiring property owners may be liable for contractor discharges (Central San statement, Contra Costa).
