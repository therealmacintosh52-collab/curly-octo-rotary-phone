## 2. Recommended categories for a mobile service-area business
Exact Google category names are not confirmed from Google's own list; the picker in the profile dashboard is authoritative. Verify each name there.
- "Auto dent removal service" appears as a live category on other PDR profiles [src https://business.google.com/v/one-up-dent-repair/014204419018884769857/3869/_/fullmenu | 2026-10-15 | "listed as an Auto Dent Removal Service"; same for PEAK Paintless Dent Repair].
- "Car detailing service" cited as detailing primary by SEO sources [src https://localsearchforum.com/threads/cant-find-out-primary-category-for-auto-detailing-business.62444/latest | 2026-10-15 | "Your primary category must be 'Car detailing service.'"]. Third-party, low authority.
- One primary + up to nine secondary [src https://daltonluka.com/blog/auto-repair-shop-google-my-business-categories | 2026-10-15 | "one primary category and up to nine secondary categories"]. Third-party; UNVERIFIED against Google help.

**Recommended primary: Auto dent removal service.** Reason: the highest-ticket service, and only one clearly mobile PDR rival surfaced (single-search observation, not density). If the first 90 days are mostly detailing, make "Car detailing service" primary instead and keep dent removal secondary. Owner decides; there is one primary.
Secondary (pick only what is truly offered and in the picker; 3-5 is realistic, 9 is the ceiling):
1. Car detailing service (core for detail + coating)
2. A distinct auto/mobile detailing variant, only if the picker has one
3. Car wash, only if wash-only jobs are offered
4. Window tinting service, only if offered
5. Vehicle wrap / paint protection film category, only if offered (no source confirms the name)
Exclude auto body shop and auto repair shop (misleading without body or mechanical work).
Ceramic coating has no dedicated category found: [src https://clicksgeek.com/auto-detailing-seo/ | 2026-10-15 | "Ceramic Coating Application ... belong in the Services section"]. List it under Services.
I found no source supporting 9 relevant categories; padding risks relevance dilution [UNVERIFIED opinion].

## 3. Google service-area business rules (Google help pages seen in results)
- No-storefront business must hide its address; once hidden only the service area shows [src https://support.google.com/business/answer/10514137?hl=en | 2026-10-15 | "once the address is hidden, your Business Profile only shows your service area"]. Toggle: Edit profile > Location > "Show business address to customers" off.
- Area limit about 2 hrs drive; up to 20 service areas [src https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en | 2026-10-15 | "shouldn't be more than about 2 hours of driving time"; "up to 20 service areas"].
- One profile for the metro area; duplicates risk suspension [src https://support.google.com/business/answer/10514137 | 2026-10-15 | "create only one Business Profile for the metropolitan area"].
- Virtual office not allowed unless staffed [src https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177 | 2026-10-15 | "can't list a 'virtual' office unless that office is staffed"]. Using a home address for verification is third-party only [UNVERIFIED on Google].
- Countries/states barred as service areas (June 2025): third-party only [src https://ppc.land/google-bans-countries-and-states-from-business-profile-service-areas/ | 2026-10-15] [UNVERIFIED on Google].
- Implication: one profile, not one per city; use website city pages. 14 target cities exceed nothing (limit 20), but all must be within ~2 hrs of the base [UNVERIFIED, base address unknown].
