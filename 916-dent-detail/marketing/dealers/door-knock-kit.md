# Dealer Door-Knock Kit

Status: DRAFT. Owner executes in person. Contacting anyone is a hard gate: the owner decides when and where. This kit does not contact anyone. Retrieved 2026-10-15.

Targets come from `marketing/research/dealer-targets.md` (public business info only): Roseville Auto Mall (Automall Dr), Folsom Auto Mall, Elk Grove auto cluster, Hanlees (Davis), Maita, Niello stores, Folsom Lake group, rental at SMF. Common ownership between same-name stores is UNVERIFIED; do not pitch a "group deal" until confirmed. Salvage auctions (Copart) are low fit. No dealer wholesale rate was sourced: owner sets prices (`wholesale-price-sheet-template.md`).

## Who to ask for
Used-car manager, reconditioning (recon) manager, fleet manager or service director. Never pitch a salesperson on the floor. Ask the receptionist: "Who handles reconditioning or cosmetic work on used inventory?"

## 30-second script (say, do not read)
```
Hi, I am [OWNER NAME] with 916 Dent & Detail. We are a mobile dent removal and detailing crew. We fix door dings and dents on your lot, no body shop, no repaint, no pulling cars off the line. I am not selling today. Could I leave a one-page rate sheet and do one car for free? [OWNER: confirm you offer a trial; if not, ask for a two-minute look at the rate sheet] Who is the best person for recon?
```
Rules: smile, name, 30 seconds, one question, leave a card and the sheet. Aim for the decision maker's name and a text/email permission, not a sale.

## Objection handling
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We have a guy." | `Great. We are overflow when he is busy or backed up. Keep our card; a ding photo gets you a price in minutes.` |
| "We use a body shop." | `Body shops usually repaint and keep the car for days. We work on the lot, no repaint. For paint-intact dents it saves time. Send us a photo and compare.` |
| "Send me an email." | `Happy to. What is the best email and may I also text you a photo-quote link?` (get consent, see legal note) |
| "Price?" | `Per panel, with a clear rate sheet. Here it is. [OWNER: hand rate sheet]. Pricing depends on dent size and count.` Never quote a dent price without seeing it. |
| "Are you insured?" | `[OWNER: only if true] Yes. I can send a certificate of insurance.` If not yet: `I will get you a certificate before we start any work.` Do not claim coverage you do not have. |
| "Do you do this for free?" | `The trial car is on us. After that, standard rates.` [OWNER: remove if no trial] |
| "Not now." | `No problem. Can I check back in two weeks?` (then log and follow Day 14). |
| "We don't allow outside vendors." | `Understood. Who handles vendor approvals? I can send a COI and W-9 if that helps.` |
| "How fast?" | `It depends on the dent. We give a time with the price. Most dealer jobs are on-lot.` [OWNER: do not promise a per-car time without evidence] |

## Safety and etiquette
- Park off the customer parking spaces; ask the receptionist first.
- Do not walk into service bays, wash bays or lot rows without permission.
- Respect "No Soliciting" signs: leave and mail the rate sheet instead.
- Do not take photos of inventory, customers or staff without permission.
- Do not touch or lean on cars. Never open vehicles.
- Heat: Sacramento afternoons can be triple-digit (typical about 23-24 days a year since 1990, CBS/ABC10 per `research/local-facts.md`). Carry water, visit mornings 9-11am, avoid Saturdays and month-end.
- Walk lot traffic with attention; keep to marked walkways.
- Thank everyone, leave nothing behind, no pressure.

## Legal notes (informational, not legal advice; confirm with a counsel before sending)
- Email: the CAN-SPAM Act applies fully to business-to-business email. Include a valid postal address and a clear opt-out, honor opt-outs within 10 business days (FTC, https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business, retrieved 2026-10-15; the 10-day figure was seen in a mirror copy, confirm on ftc.gov).
- Text: marketing texts to a person's mobile phone need prior express written consent when sent with an autodialer/automated system. The FCC's one-to-one consent rule was vacated by the Eleventh Circuit and not in force (Steptoe, https://www.steptoe.com/en/news-publications/one-to-none-the-fcc-and-eleventh-circuit-issue-last-minute-rulings-gutting-the-one-to-one-tcpa-consent-rule.html, 2026-10-15). Revocation rules became effective April 11, 2025 and opt-outs must be honored via any reasonable method, with do-not-call requests processed within 10 business days (ActiveProspect summary, https://activeprospect.com/blog/fcc-tcpa/, 2026-10-15; third-party; a late-2025 FCC effort to revisit parts of the rules was mentioned, so re-check).
- California: Business and Professions Code section 17538.41 governs text message advertisements; current law requires consent before texting (Assembly Privacy Committee analysis https://apcp.assembly.ca.gov/media/1077, 2026-10-15). AB 1865 (would bar text ads 9pm-9am Pacific) was pending as of 2026-05-16, status unconfirmed since (https://www.govbuddy.com/california/bills/ab-1865/). Stay inside 9am-8pm anyway. No search result addressed CIPA; verify with counsel.
- Practical rule: **do not text a dealer contact unless they gave their number and agreed to texts in writing** (a written yes by email, or a signed card line "OK to text me"). Whether the TCPA covers business-to-business texts is not settled in our sources: treat it as applying.
- Include "Reply STOP to opt out" in every marketing text. Log every opt-out the day it arrives and never contact that person again.
- Cold calls: federal Do Not Call rules apply to residential numbers; business-to-business calls differ (not verified here). Do not cold call for this kit.

## Follow-up sequence (day 0, 2, 7, 14) with compliant opt-outs
Preconditions: you met the contact and got name + role + permission for email (and for text only if they agreed in writing). Log each touch. Copy for email is in `dealer-email-templates.md`.
| Day | Channel | Purpose | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Email (+ text only if consented) | Thank you + rate sheet | template E1 / T1 |
| 2 | Text if consented, else email | Offer a trial car or a photo quote | T2 / E2 |
| 7 | Email | Social proof (real jobs only) + COI offer | E3 |
| 14 | Email (text if consented) | Last check, stay in touch | E4 / T4 |
After day 14: monthly check-in at most, or stop. Remove anyone who opted out. Never message the same person more than once a week.

Text copy (only to opted-in numbers):
```
T1 (Day 0): Hi [FIRST NAME], [OWNER NAME] at 916 Dent & Detail. Thanks for your time today. Rate sheet sent to your email. Reply STOP to opt out.
T2 (Day 2): Hi [FIRST NAME], happy to do one dent on a lot car so you can see the work. Photo of the car works. Reply STOP to opt out.
T4 (Day 14): Hi [FIRST NAME], last note from me. If dents pile up on the lot, I am a text away. Reply STOP to opt out.
```
