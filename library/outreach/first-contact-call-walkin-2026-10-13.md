Say, then stop talking: **"Would it be okay if I text you the details? One text, and you can reply STOP."**
- Spoken yes: log it in your Sheets CRM right away, then send the text in (c). Columns: `date | time | who said yes (name, role) | business | offer A2/A3/A4 | number from card | text sent Y/N | stop/never-contact`.

- Text goes only after that spoken yes, once, from your own phone, by hand, within 1 hour of the call. A yes to details is not a yes to buy; take no payment until they agree in writing to the terms (kit terms, C7).
