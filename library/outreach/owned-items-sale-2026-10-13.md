## Pricing from comps (method, 4 steps)
1. Search the exact model on eBay and on Marketplace/OfferUp in the Elk Grove-Sacramento area. On eBay filter to Sold/Completed items (menu name may differ).
2. Write down the last 5 sold prices (ignore the highest and lowest). Median = comp.
3. List at comp + 10% (room to negotiate). Floor = 70% of comp. (10% and 70% are growth ASSUMPTIONS, not market facts.)
4. H48 rule (red-team card): fewer than 3 buyer messages after 48 h = one 20% price cut. $0 at H72 = card killed.
Electronics (I-016): screenshot one in-app trade-in quote if a retailer app gives one without spending money. Compare to your local comp. Take the better cash number. Store credit is not cash; do not count it as a sale.
