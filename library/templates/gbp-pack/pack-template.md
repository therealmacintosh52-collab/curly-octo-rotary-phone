## 8. How to paste this in (phone, ~10 min)
1. Open the Google Maps app (or search your
   business name in Google) while signed in
   to the Google account that manages your
   business.
2. Open your business profile. Look for
   "Edit profile" or "Edit business". Button
   names change over time; if you cannot
   find it, text me a screenshot.
3. Description: tap the description box,
   delete the old text, paste section 1,
   save.
4. Categories: open the category setting,
   pick the section 2 names from Google's
   list, save.
5. Services: open the services setting, add
   each section 3 service, save.
6. Hours and details: compare section 6 with
   what is shown. Change what differs.
7. Photos: add the section 7 photos.
8. Posts: look for "Add update" or "Post".
   Paste one post from section 5 a week,
   with a photo.
9. If an edit does not show right away,
   check again the next day.
