# GBP pack intake, 10 minutes (EXP-A2)

Internal. For the owner. Not sent to the client.
Do this only after the payment shows (C5).

## Part A. Screenshots (3 min, before you ask)
Open Google Maps on your phone.
Search the business by its exact name.
Screenshot each of these. Missing = fine;
a screenshot of "nothing there" is data.

- [ ] A1 The top of the card: name,
      category line, rating line as shown
- [ ] A2 Hours, as shown (or "no hours")
- [ ] A3 The "About" / description part,
      if there is one
- [ ] A4 Photos tab: the first screen
      (we record what is shown, not a count
      we guess)
- [ ] A5 Services or menu part, if shown
- [ ] A6 Website / Call / Directions
      buttons, as shown
- [ ] A7 Reviews sorted by newest, first
      screen (for the posts tone only; we
      never quote a review without asking)

Write nothing down from memory.
Only what the screenshot shows.

## Part B. The 5 facts (5 min, by text)
These match the "5 quick facts" in
growth's follow-up text. Copy-paste:

```
Thanks! 5 quick ones so the pack is right:
1) Your services, up to 10, in your words
2) Hours, and the area you serve
3) What makes you different, in your words
   (years in business only if you want it said)
4) Anything you do NOT want said
5) A booking or website link to include, if any
```

- [ ] B1 Services list (up to 10)
- [ ] B2 Hours + service area
- [ ] B3 Difference, in their words
- [ ] B4 Do-not-say list
- [ ] B5 Booking / website link

## Part C. Optional (2 min, only if they
are happy to answer)
- [ ] C1 Payment methods they take
- [ ] C2 Languages spoken
- [ ] C3 Busy season, if any (for posts)
- [ ] C4 OK to use photos they send? (yes/no)
- [ ] C5 Who is named as owner/team, if
      anyone (only with their OK)

## Rules
- Their words beat ours. Quote them.
- No answer = the line stays out of the
  pack. Never fill a gap with a guess.
- Do not ask for their Google login, ever.
- Keep their answers in this chat only.
  Do not commit client answers to the repo.
