## 2. Scoring

Formula: Score = 0.30*TTFD + 0.20*Ceiling + 0.20*Hours + 0.15*Legal + 0.15*Capital. Each factor 1-5 (5 best).
Weights reflect the owner's stated priorities: first dollar within ~10 days weighted highest; the other four are the constraints named in the task.

| (b) Reselling | 4 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 1.2+0.4+0.4+0.45+0.45 = 2.90 |
| (c) Trendlift dropship | 1 | 2 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 0.3+0.4+0.2+0.45+0.15 = 1.50 |

Reasons (every score is a judgment; underlying numbers are tagged above):
- (a) TTFD 3: no outreach is allowed without an approval ID, no warm lead is known, prospect list has no confirmed "no website" business, and the pitch anchor (did past clients pay?) is UNKNOWN. A reply plus Stripe-enabled deposit inside 10 days is plausible but not demonstrated. Ceiling 3: $10k/mo needs ~100 clients at $100/mo or ~13 builds at $750/mo; build labor is agent-done but sales conversations are the owner's. Hours 4: agents build and QA; owner approves and talks. Legal 4: services, no inventory, needs a local license (L1, L6 unknown). Capital 5: domain $12-25/yr (a7 UNVERIFIED) plus existing Vercel/GitHub connectors.
- (b) TTFD 4: local pickup is fee-free (b7 UNVERIFIED) and a sale can close in days, but only if the owner already holds sellable inventory (UNKNOWN per CONTEXT.md). Ceiling 2: blogs say $500-$2,000/mo part-time (b2 UNVERIFIED); the one full-time anecdote is $2,600/mo net (b3). Hours 2: sourcing, photos, listing, meetups all fall on the owner; agents cannot touch physical goods. Legal 3: permit and sales tax once regular (b8 VERIFIED), plus platform TOS. Capital 3: stock cash is UNKNOWN; tools are free.
- (c) Scores follow c1-c4 and the repo post-mortem (failure-patterns FP-02, FP-03, FP-05).

Sensitivity: swap (a) TTFD to 2 and (b) TTFD to 5 and the scores are (a) 3.35 vs (b) 3.20, so the order holds, but the margin is thin. The (a) vs (b) gap closes entirely if the owner confirms inventory on hand and (a) has no warm lead.
