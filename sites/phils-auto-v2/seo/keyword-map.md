## Cannibalization checks

- **Home vs `/services/auto-repair/`.** Both could chase "auto repair Lodi". Resolution: home owns "auto repair Lodi CA" (brand + category); the service page owns "mechanic Lodi" and "auto repair shop Lodi". The service page's H1 says "shop", the home H1 says "repair". Internal anchors follow the split (home links to it as "our auto repair shop").
- **Diesel vs fleet.** Overlap on "diesel truck repair". Diesel page owns engine-type queries (Cummins, Duramax, Powerstroke); fleet page owns business queries (fleet, commercial, work trucks, downtime). Cross-link once each way.
- **Car diagnostics page vs check-engine advice post.** Service page is transactional ("diagnosis in Lodi"); the post answers "what does it mean". Post links down to the service page in the first 100 words and again at the end.
- **Service areas vs city pages.** No separate city pages at launch (thin-content risk). One service-areas page with a real paragraph per town. Revisit only if Search Console shows town-level impressions.
- **Spanish page.** Own keyword family in Spanish; `hreflang` pairs it with the home page only.

## Things not to do

- No "near me" in titles (it reads as spam); keep it in body copy and the FAQ schema.
- No invented review counts. The 4.4 / 83 figure comes from `build.py` in v1 and must be re-checked against the live Google profile before launch.
- No city stuffing in the footer beyond the NAP and the one service-areas link.
