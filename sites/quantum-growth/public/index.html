Straight answers

The questions everyone asks

Including the awkward ones. If something is not here, call and ask.

I already have a website.

Most of our clients did. The website is not really what you are buying — being found is. If your current site works, keep it and take a plan that leaves it alone. We will tell you honestly if replacing it would not change anything.

I'm already paying someone for this.

Then ask them two questions before you do anything else. Whose name is your domain registered in? And are you the Owner on your Google Business Profile, or just a Manager? If the answer is the vendor's name, you do not own the two things your business runs on. That is worth knowing regardless of whether you ever work with us.

How do I know it's actually working?

Your monthly report is Google's own data — calls from your listing, direction requests, form submissions, reviews gained. You can log into your profile and verify every number yourself. We are not the source of the scoreboard.

How long until I see something?

Profile work — photos, categories, posts, reviews — can move within weeks. Ranking for competitive searches takes months, and depends on how strong your competitors already are. Anyone promising page one in thirty days is either lying or about to do something that gets your listing suspended.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel whenever, no notice period. Paying for a year up front gets you one month free, and you can still leave — unused full months are refunded. If you would rather not stop entirely, you can pause for up to two months a year at $29 a month, or drop to a smaller plan at any time.

What happens if I leave?

You keep everything. The domain, website, Google profile and email are already in your name, so there is nothing for us to switch off or hand over. After six months you just stop paying. Inside six months there is a fee of $100 to $500 depending on the plan, which recovers part of the build we gave you free. Annual plans get unused full months back.

You're not in my town. Does that matter?

Not for the work. Your Google profile, your reviews and your website do not care where the person managing them sits, and everything is verifiable by you from your own logins. What you would lose with a local agency is someone who can walk in. What you gain here is one person who picks up the phone rather than a ticket queue.

Why are you cheaper than the company that cold-called me?

Because they are paying for a sales floor and you are not. A commissioned rep, a manager, a retention team and a marketing budget all sit inside that monthly price. We do not carry that cost, so we do not have to charge you for it.

Who writes the content and the posts?

We do, using what you tell us about the business during setup. You approve anything you want to approve. Nothing goes out under your name that you have asked to see first.

What if I want changes later?

Text edits are unlimited on every plan and are not billed. Bigger changes — new pages, new sections — are included from Growth up, and quoted flat-rate below that.
