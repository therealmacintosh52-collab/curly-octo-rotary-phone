About Phil's Auto and Fleet Repair

A locally owned repair shop in Lodi, California, built around one idea: tell people the truth about their vehicles.

A dealership alternative for Lodi drivers

Phil's Auto and Fleet Repair is a locally owned shop serving Lodi drivers and fleets from a shop at 103 E Elm St, Lodi, CA 95240. We work on domestic and import vehicles, diesel trucks and commercial fleets — everything from a routine oil change to a drivability fault three other shops couldn't pin down.

What we're known for locally isn't a slogan. Customers on Google, Yelp and neighborhood boards keep describing the same experience: a vehicle that was misdiagnosed elsewhere, finally diagnosed correctly here, without a list of extra services attached to the bill.

How we work

  • Diagnose first. A trouble code is a starting point, not an answer. We test until we can point at the failure.
  • Explain it plainly. You should understand what failed and why before you spend money on it.
  • Quote before we start. Nothing gets repaired without your approval — including anything we find along the way.
  • Separate urgent from optional. We'll tell you what can safely wait. That's not lost revenue; that's why you come back.
  • Respect your time. Realistic turnaround estimates, and a call if anything changes.
