Why misdiagnosis comes up so often

A large share of the reviews mentioning us describe the same story: a vehicle that had already been to another shop, sometimes more than one, with parts already replaced and the original symptom still there.

That happens when a shop treats a trouble code as a diagnosis. The code tells you which circuit reported a fault — not which component caused it. Replacing the most likely part is faster than testing, and when it works, nobody notices. When it doesn't, the customer pays for the guess.

We test instead. It takes longer up front and it's the reason people drive past closer shops to get here.
