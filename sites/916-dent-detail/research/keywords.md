Retrieved 2026-10-18. Mobile PDR + detailing, Sacramento CA, service-area business.

## Read this first: limits of the data

- **No keyword-volume tool was available.** No search volumes, CPCs or difficulty scores appear here, and none should be inferred. Every "competitiveness" value is an **ESTIMATE** from what the search tool returned (who ranks, what kind of pages, how many local specialists).
- **Google autocomplete was not directly observable.** The search tool returns summarised result lists, not suggest dropdowns. Query variants below come from (a) competitor page titles and listing names surfaced in results, (b) topics the result summaries repeatedly covered (cost, time, PDR vs body shop, insurance, limits), (c) the owner's own city list and service names, and (d) standard trade vocabulary. Variants from (c) and (d) are **hypotheses to validate** in Google Search Console, Google Ads Keyword Planner (free with an account, the owner creates it) or a rank tracker after launch.
- **WebFetch was blocked** (DNS failure on every domain, proxy 403), so competitor H1s and titles could not be read directly. Only titles and snippets that appeared in search results are used.
- SERP landscape observed (search results, Oct 2026): "paintless dent repair Sacramento" returned mostly directory/aggregator listings (Lantern, ZoomInfo, Dribbble) plus the CARSTAR Capitol Collision page; the specialist PDR shops visible were Primetech PDR, The Dent Tent, Dent Shoppe, Bullseye Dent Removal (Roseville/Rocklin). "Mobile detailing Sacramento ceramic coating" returned Google Business listings, poyst/bestprosintown-type directories and small mobile detailers (Coastal Detail, Nor Cal Mobile Detailing Pros, Hunter's, Apex Detail Mobile, Sacramento Car Detailing). Few strong own-domain pages appeared, which suggests (ESTIMATE) the organic field is soft and map pack plus directories dominate.

Competitiveness key (ESTIMATE): Low = few or weak dedicated local pages seen; Med = several specialists or one strong body-shop/brand page; High = many established local pages plus national brands and directories.

Page-type key: HOME, SVC (service page), CITY (city page, only if rule 5 is met), HUB (service-area), B2B (dealer/fleet page), GUIDE (guide/blog), FAQ, COST (pricing page), HAIL.

## 6. Question queries (informational; GUIDE or FAQ ownership)

Themes confirmed as recurring in the SERP summaries: cost, time, PDR limits, PDR vs body shop, insurance, mobile fees.

- How much does paintless dent repair cost? (national ranges seen: $50-$150 per dent; $75-$150 small, $150-$350 for 2-4 in; $100-$400 single standard dent; extra $50-$100 mobile fee. SEO-style sources, inconsistent. Do NOT publish as 916's prices.) Owner: COST page, with the owner's own ranges once supplied.
- How long does PDR take? (30-90 min single small dent; 2-6 h larger; hail 1-3 days per sources.) Owner: FAQ + SVC PDR.
- Can all dents be fixed with PDR? (No: sharp creases, stretched or cracked paint, panel edges, thick metal.) Owner: SVC PDR "what it can't fix".
- Does paintless dent repair work on hail damage?
- Does insurance cover dent repair? (comprehensive vs collision) Owner: GUIDE insurance + FAQ.
- Will PDR hurt my paint or resale value?
- Is mobile dent repair as good as a shop?
- How much is mobile car detailing in Sacramento? (a 2026 Elk Grove page showed ~$150-$275 entry full detail; low-quality source, unverified)
- Does ceramic coating protect against Sacramento sun? (UV and 100F+ summers are vendor claims; no independent Sacramento data found.)
- How long does ceramic coating last?
- Do I need paint correction before ceramic coating? (Sources say coating jobs usually begin with correction.)
- Is it legal to wash a car in the driveway / mobile detailing runoff Sacramento? (City of Sacramento stormwater prohibits discharge of wash water to storm drains; fines up to $5,000/day per county ordinance, per City brochure aimed at commercial washing.) Owner: eco/runoff section + FAQ.
- Do mobile detailers need water and power from me?
- How much does it cost to have a dealership dent fixed vs PDR?

## 11. Gaps and opportunities (ESTIMATE, from limited SERP evidence)

- No dedicated "send a photo for a price" page seen among Sacramento PDR competitors (only call/text quotes mentioned). A photo-quote funnel is a differentiator and a long-tail target.
- No Sacramento-local price guide seen for PDR; national cost pages dominate.
- No Sacramento-local mobile dealer-recon page seen.
- Competitor listings are thin on proof; most evidence comes via aggregator profiles. A well-structured, fact-rich own site with real photos can stand out.
- Water reclaim / stormwater compliance wording (owner spec) is a legitimate local trust angle, but any "reclaim" claim needs the owner's confirmation of actual equipment.
