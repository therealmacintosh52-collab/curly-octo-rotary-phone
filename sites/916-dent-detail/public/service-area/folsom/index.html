Workplace lots

Can you work at a large campus or office lot in Folsom?

Often, with the right permission. Intel has a campus in Folsom, off Prairie City Road and Highway 50, and large campuses have their own rules about vendors on site. Water rule

Does Folsom have rules about washing cars?

Yes. Sacramento County lists Folsom among the cities with its own stormwater ordinance, and says businesses may not let wash water of any kind run into storm drains. Dent removal uses no water. For detailing at your driveway or lot, we explain how we handle water before you book. See mobile detailing.

Sources

  • Sacramento County, stormwater program FAQ

Retail

Where do door dings happen in Folsom?

Anywhere cars share a lot. Greenback Lane is one of Folsom's retail streets, and a ding from a neighboring car in a shopping-center lot is the everyday case for paintless repair. If the paint is intact and the dent is shallow and rounded, it is often a good candidate. See paintless dent repair for the method and its limits.
