Can this dent be fixed without paint? A plain self-check

Many dents can be fixed without paint when they are shallow-to-moderate, rounded and the paint is unbroken. Sharp creases, stretched or cracked paint, and some edges usually can't. Run the six checks below on your dent, then send a photo and we tell you which side it is on.

Self-check

How do I tell if my dent can be fixed without paint?

Run these six checks. You are looking for the pattern, not a perfect score.

  1. Is the paint cracked, chipped or flaking in the dent?

    If yes, paintless repair is probably not the right tool. Moving metal under broken paint can lift or crack more of it.

  2. Is the dent round and shallow, like a shallow bowl, or is it a line or fold?

    Round and shallow is the easy case. A sharp line or fold is a crease, which often needs other repair.

  3. Do you see white, crazed or stretched-looking marks in the paint?

    That suggests the metal has stretched. It can limit how far a dent comes out.

  4. Where is it?

    The flat middle of a door or fender is easiest. Panel edges, body lines and braced areas are harder, and some are not PDR jobs.

  5. What is the panel made of?

    Steel is more forgiving. Aluminum can be judged case by case. If you do not know, tell us the make, model and year.

  6. How large is it, and how many are there?

    Size matters, but shape and paint matter more. A big, soft dent can be easier than a small, sharp one.

The shapes

What do fixable and not-fixable dents look like?

Shallow and round

The paint is intact and the metal has a smooth dip. This is the usual PDR candidate.

Sharp crease

The metal is folded into a line. Often not a PDR job.

Stretched or cracked

The paint or metal has been pulled past its limit. Usually needs conventional repair.

Ask us

Panel edges, aluminum, braced areas and dents with a scratch through them. These depend on the details, so send a photo.

If not

What if my dent isn't a paintless repair job?

Then we say so after looking at your photos, and the next stop is a body shop. That is a normal answer, not a failure of the method: paintless repair keeps the original paint, and it can only do that when the paint and the metal allow it. Structural damage and anything near airbag sensors always belongs in a body shop. For the comparison, read PDR or body shop.

Questions

Questions about fixable dents

Can a dent on a door edge be fixed?

Sometimes. Edges are harder to reach and the metal is stiffer there. It depends on the dent and the paint, so send a close photo.

What about a dent with a scratch in it?

Paintless repair can address the dent. A scratch that goes through the paint is a separate paint repair. If the scratch is only in the clear coat, paint correction may help.

Is a bigger dent harder to fix?

Not necessarily. A large, soft, rounded dent can be easier than a small sharp one. Shape and paint decide more than size.
