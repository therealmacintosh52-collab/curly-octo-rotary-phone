What changes the price of a dent repair

A dent repair price is driven by the size, depth and sharpness of the dent, how many dents and panels are involved, the panel material, how reachable the spot is and the condition of the paint. We don't publish a price list, because a number without a photo is a guess. Send photos and we price your car.

The drivers

How much does paintless dent repair cost in Sacramento?

We cannot give an honest number without a photo, and nobody can who has not seen the dent. Two dents that look the same in a description can be a very different amount of work. These are the things that decide it:

Why no price list

Why don't you publish prices?

Because a published price per dent would be an average of very different jobs, and the average would be wrong for almost everyone. Prices you see online for dent repair are usually that kind of average. We would rather look at your dent and tell you what it will take.

What you get

What does a price from a photo include?

We tell you whether the dent looks like a PDR candidate, a price for your car, and what could change it once we see the paint in person. A photo price is an estimate, not a quote, until the vehicle has been looked at. If the dent turns out to be harder than the photos showed, we say so before we start.

How to

How do I get an accurate price quickly?

  1. Take four photos

    The whole panel, the dent straight on, the dent from a low angle with light across it, and a close-up of the paint at the dent. The photo guide has the drawings.

  2. Tell us the car and the city

    Year, make, model and the city the car will be in. You do not need to give an address.

  3. Send them

    Text them to (916) 868-2447 or use the photo quote form.

What about dealerships and fleets?

Dealers, fleets, rental lots and body shops are quoted per vehicle after we look at photos. Tell us how many vehicles and where they are. See dealers and fleets.

Questions

Questions about price

Will you give me a price by text?

Yes. Send photos and we tell you what we can do and what it will cost for your car, along with anything that could change the price on inspection.

Is hail priced differently?

Hail is priced per vehicle after we see the dents, because count and panels decide it. See hail damage repair.

Is a paintless repair always cheaper than a body shop?

Not always. It often is when a dent qualifies, because it skips sanding, filling and painting. When it does not qualify, a body shop is the right answer. See PDR or body shop.
