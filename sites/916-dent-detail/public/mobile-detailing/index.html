Paint Deep-Clean

What is Paint Deep-Clean (paint decontamination)?

A wash removes loose dirt. It does not remove what clings to the paint: iron particles from brake dust, road tar, tree sap, overspray and mineral water spots. Decontamination is the step that deals with those. Done before polishing or coating, it gives the next step a clean surface.

A quick way to check your own car: put your hand in a thin plastic bag and glide it over clean, dry paint. If it feels gritty instead of smooth, something is stuck to the surface.

Tree sap and water spots

Fresh sap is easier to lift than sap that has baked on through a hot stretch, and mineral spots are easier before they etch. If you see either, tell us when you text and send a close photo. Some etched spots will have to be polished out instead, which is paint correction.

Your part

What do I need to provide for a mobile detail?

It depends on the job, so tell us where the car will be parked and whether the spot has an outdoor tap, an outlet and some shade. We confirm what your job needs before we book it, and we explain how we handle water on your job.

Water is a real question for any mobile cleaning in this area. Sacramento County says businesses may not let wash water of any kind, even soapy water, run into storm drains, and Sacramento, Elk Grove, Folsom, Citrus Heights and Rancho Cordova each have their own stormwater rules. Dent removal uses no water at all. See the county's stormwater program page.

Questions about mobile detailing

How long does a mobile detail take?

It depends on the car, its condition and what you want done, so we do not quote a time in advance. Send a photo and the goal and we give you a plan.

Can you detail my car before I sell it?

Yes, and it helps presentation. It does not hide dents or deep scratches, so a dent check first can matter more. Start with dent removal if there are dings.
