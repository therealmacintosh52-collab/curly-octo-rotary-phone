Dent Removal (no paint, no body shop)

Paintless dent repair that comes to you, no paint and no body shop

Paintless dent repair, or PDR, removes a dent by working the metal back into shape from behind the panel, so the original paint stays. It suits shallow-to-moderate dents where the paint is intact. We come to your home, workplace or lot, and we tell you from photos whether yours qualifies.

The method

What is paintless dent repair, and how does it work?

Paintless dent repair (PDR) removes a dent by working the metal back into shape from behind the panel, or from around it, in many small pushes. The technician reads the dent by how reflections bend across the surface and keeps going until the reflections run straight again. Nothing is sanded, filled or painted, so the car keeps its original paint.

That is also why PDR has rules. It needs paint that is still intact, and metal that has not been folded or stretched too far. When a dent meets those conditions, PDR is usually the quieter, less invasive repair. When it doesn't, the honest answer is a body shop.

Process

What happens when you book a dent repair with us?

  1. You send photos

    Two to four photos: the whole panel, the dent straight on, the dent at a low angle with light across it, and a close-up if the paint looks cracked. The photo guide shows each shot.

  2. We look and answer

    We tell you whether the dent looks like a PDR candidate, what is likely to drive the price on your car, and what happens next. If it is not a PDR job, we say so here, before anyone drives out.

  3. We come to you

    Your home, workplace or lot, in one of the places we serve. Tell us where the car will be parked so we can confirm the spot works.

  4. You check the panel

    Before we leave, you look at the finished panel with us. If the dent turned out harder than the photos showed, we tell you that too.

Door dings

Can you fix a door ding or a parking-lot dent without repainting?

Often, yes. A door ding or parking-lot dent is a common job for paintless repair: a small to medium dent, usually rounded, in paint that has not cracked. Dings on the flat part of a door or fender tend to be reachable. Dings on a panel edge, on a braced area or right on a body line are harder, and some of those are not PDR jobs.

If the paint has a crack, a chip or a white, crazed look inside the dent, tell us when you text. That is the detail that most often changes the answer.

Limits

What can't paintless dent repair fix?

PDR is not a cure for every dent. These are the cases where it usually is not the right tool:

Sharp crease

Metal folded into a line does not push back to a smooth surface. Many creases need conventional repair.

Stretched or cracked paint

White stress marks, cracks or chips mean the paint has already failed. Moving the metal can make that worse.

  • Some panel edges, braced areas and spots with no access from behind
  • Aluminum panels, which can take more care and are judged case by case
  • Structural damage and areas near airbag sensors, which belong in a body shop

Our plain self-check goes through these one by one. We tell you after photos, or in person, if yours is not a PDR job.

Price

What changes the price of paintless dent repair?

Dent size, depth and sharpness, how many dents are on the panel, which panels they are on, what the panel is made of, how easy the spot is to reach, and the condition of the paint. There is no honest way to price that from a description, which is why we start from photos. The full list of price drivers has no numbers because a number without a photo would be a guess.

Hail

Is hail damage different?

Hail leaves many dents at once, often shallow and round, the kind PDR handles well, but it is a bigger job. We cover it on its own page: hail damage repair.

Questions

Questions about dent removal

Will paintless dent repair damage my paint?

The method does not sand, fill or repaint, so paint that is intact stays original. Paint that is already cracked, chipped or brittle can crack further when the metal is moved, which is why we ask for a close-up photo before we say yes.

How long does it take?

We do not quote a time without seeing the dent, because it depends on how large it is, how many there are and where they sit. Send photos and we give you a plan for your car.

Do I need a garage or shade?

Tell us where the car will be when you text: driveway, office lot, garage. We confirm whether the spot works for the job.

Do you work on aluminum panels?

Panel material changes both the work and the price. Tell us the make, model and which panel, and we say whether we take it.

Is it cheaper than a body shop?

When a dent is a PDR candidate it skips the sanding, filling and painting steps where body work adds time and cost. That is a general pattern, not a promise: photos decide. See PDR or body shop.
