Interior Reset

Interior car detailing: pet hair, spills and the smell that stays

Interior detailing is a deep clean of everything you touch and sit on: seats, carpets, door panels, vents and the seams between them. It goes after pet hair, spills and odors, not just surface dust. Tell us what happened in the car and send a photo, and we say what we can do.

Scope

What is included in an interior detail?

Interior detailing is a deep clean of what you sit on and touch: seats, carpets and mats, door panels, the dash and console, vents, cupholders and the seams where crumbs and sand collect. It is slower than a quick vacuum because it works into the places a vacuum doesn't reach.

  • Seats and carpets, fabric or leather
  • Door panels, dash, console and trim
  • Vents, cupholders and seams
  • Mats, and the spots under them

Problems

“Reset” is the goal: back to a clean starting point. We tell you from photos which marks we expect to lift and which may stay.

Before you book

What should I tell you before the interior detail?

  • Fabric or leather seats
  • What spilled and roughly when
  • Pets, smoke, or anything that smells
  • Any sign of water getting in, such as damp carpet or a musty smell. That is a different problem from dirt and we will say so.
  • Where the car will be, and whether there is an outlet and some shade

Take a few photos of the worst areas in daylight. The photo guide works for interiors too.

Your part

Should I clean the car out first?

Please take out personal items, papers and anything valuable, and bag any rubbish. You do not need to vacuum or scrub first; that is the work. Child seats come out or stay as you prefer, and we tell you if one needs to be removed to reach what is under it.

Questions

Questions about interior detailing

Can you remove pet hair?

Most of it, yes. Hair woven into carpet can leave a trace. Send a photo of the worst spot and we say what to expect.

Can you remove the smell?

It depends on the cause. If the source is on the surface, cleaning usually helps. If it has soaked into foam or padding, the odor may only be reduced. We say which we expect from your description.

Do you clean leather?

Leather and fabric need different care. Tell us what your seats are made of and we say what is right for them.

Can I add an interior detail to a dent repair visit?

Ask when you text. We tell you whether the two jobs can be booked together at your spot.
