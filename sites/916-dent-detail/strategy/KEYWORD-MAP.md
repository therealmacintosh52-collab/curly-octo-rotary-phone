## 2. Cannibalization controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Home vs PDR page ("mobile dent repair" vs "paintless dent repair") | Home owns "mobile dent repair Sacramento" and brand/near-me terms; PDR page owns "paintless dent repair" and "dent removal". PDR page title and H1 do not contain "mobile dent repair" as a phrase; home title does not contain "paintless" |
| Home vs Sacramento city page | Home never lists neighborhoods. The Sacramento page never uses "paintless dent repair Sacramento" in title or H1; it targets neighborhood and at-home or at-work intent and links up to the service pages |
| PDR page vs hail page | PDR page mentions hail in one paragraph with a link; hail page owns hail terms. Hail page does not define PDR beyond one sentence with a link |
| PDR page vs "can this dent be fixed" guide | PDR page carries a short "what PDR can't fix" block (5 bullets) and links to the guide, which owns the full self-check |
| PDR page vs cost guide | PDR page lists "what affects price" in one sentence and links; the guide owns the topic |
| Detailing hub vs paint-correction / coating / interior | Hub owns "mobile car detailing" and the Paint Deep-Clean section; children own their own terms; the hub links to them with their exact keyword anchors and does not explain them in depth |
| Service pages vs city pages | City pages are about the place (landmarks, typical vehicles and lots, local conditions, regulations) and link to services; they do not re-explain a service |
| FAQ page vs service-page FAQs | FAQ page contains only cross-cutting questions (how the photo quote works, where we work, what to have ready, how to send photos); service-specific Q&A stay on their pages; no answer text is duplicated verbatim (shingle gate) |
| Guides index vs guides | Index gives one sentence per guide and owns no query |
| Photo-quote vs contact | Photo-quote owns the "send a photo" intent; contact owns call and text navigation |

## 3. Near-me and GBP

Near-me queries ("dent repair near me", "mobile detailing near me") are won by Google Business Profile proximity and reviews, not page copy. The home page supports them with the canonical NAP in crawlable text, the named-city list, and "mobile service". Page work is not a lever here; GBP, citations and reviews are (Stage 5).
