## Rules for every shot

- **Consent.** Before photographing a customer's vehicle, get a yes (text is fine) that you may use photos of the vehicle for marketing. Keep the text. For dealer and fleet vehicles ask the manager and do not show dealer branding without permission. No customer faces, no children, no house numbers, no street signs, no mail, no reflections of people in the paint.
- **Plates.** Hide every license plate: tilt the camera, cover it, or blur it before sharing. Check reflections and windscreen stickers. Blur VINs.
- **Location.** Turn off location tagging in the camera app. Strip EXIF GPS before uploading anywhere (the home address must never leak). Do not shoot in front of the owner's home.
- **No fakes.** Before and after must be the same vehicle, same spot, same angle, same light, within one visit. No edits beyond crop and light exposure. No filters, no AI enhancement, no added water beads, no dents drawn in.
- **Originals.** Shoot at the camera's highest quality. Keep originals. Deliver files named exactly as below (lowercase, hyphens).
- **Light.** Open shade or overcast for paint. For dents use a long reflection line (a strip light, a window, a ruler of light). For swirls, a single bright light at a low angle.
- **Orientation.** Landscape (3:2 or 16:9) for the site and GBP. Portrait (4:5 or 9:16) only where listed, for Instagram and TikTok.
