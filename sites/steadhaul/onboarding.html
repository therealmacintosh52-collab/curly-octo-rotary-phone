How we handle your paperwork

Your packet is the thing thieves want

Your W-9, insurance certificate and authority letter together are everything somebody needs to impersonate your company, book a load in your name and steal it — and cargo insurers routinely deny those claims. You should be careful who you send this to. Here's exactly what we do with it.

We never take documents by email or text

Only through the encrypted signing link above. If anyone claiming to be us asks you to email a W-9, it isn't us — call the number on this page.

Your W-9 is deleted once you're set up

We don't pay you, the broker or your factor does — so we have no reason to keep it. Once your broker setups are done, it's gone.

We never ask for bank details

Not by form, email or text. Your banking goes to your factor or the broker directly. Nobody at Steadhaul needs it.

Packets go through broker portals

Not as email attachments floating around inboxes. That's what brokers prefer anyway.
