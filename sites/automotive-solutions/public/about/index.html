About Automotive Solutions by Single

A family owned repair shop in Elk Grove, California, doing the same thing since 2001: telling people the truth about their vehicles.

Twenty-plus years on Elk Grove Boulevard

Automotive Solutions by Single is a family owned and operated shop at 9253 Elk Grove Blvd, Elk Grove, CA 95624, serving Elk Grove drivers since 2001. It is run by Mike and Valerie Single, and in more than two decades it has repaired thousands of vehicles for people who live and work in this town.

We provide expert repairs, service and maintenance on all makes and models of domestic and import vehicles. Our technicians are ASE certified, and we use up-to-date diagnostic equipment and quality parts on every job that comes through the bay.

How we work

  • Diagnose first. A trouble code is a starting point, not an answer. We test until we can point at the failure.
  • Explain it plainly. You should understand what failed and why before you spend money on it.
  • Quote before we start. Nothing gets repaired without your approval — including anything we find along the way.
  • Separate urgent from optional. We will tell you what can safely wait. That is not lost revenue; that is why people come back.
  • Back it in writing. Qualifying repairs carry the NAPA Peace of Mind Warranty — 24 months or 24,000 miles, nationwide.

What being a NAPA AutoCare Center means

NAPA AutoCare is a network of independent repair shops that agree to a common standard. Member shops employ ASE certified technicians, use quality parts, and honor each other's warranty work. In practice that means if something we repaired gives you trouble while you are out of town, another member shop can look after it under the same coverage.

It also means an independent business does not have to ask you to take its word for anything. You can look us up in NAPA's own directory, on the Better Business Bureau, and on Google and Yelp — all of them are linked from this site.
