Why we don't put a star rating in our page code

Plenty of small business websites publish their own star rating as structured data so a rating shows up in Google's search results. Google's guidelines say not to: a business marking up its own reviews is self-serving, and sites that do it risk losing the rich result altogether.

So the rating on this site is stated in plain text and linked to the platform that calculated it. If you want to know what people think of this shop, the honest place to look is Google and Yelp, not our own HTML.
