The short answer

Order Stark's Hot Chicken three ways: in-store pickup at 207 S Vermont Ave, delivery on DoorDash, or delivery on Uber Eats. Pickup is usually the fastest and the cheapest — delivery-platform prices are higher than in-store. Call (213) 378-0138 to order by phone.
