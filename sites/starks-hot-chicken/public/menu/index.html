The short answer

Stark's Hot Chicken serves sandos, jumbo tenders, wings, popcorn chicken, loaded fries, sides and combos. Sandos start at $12.99, tenders at $11.99, wings at $10.99 and combos run $16.99 to $19.99. Every chicken item comes in one of six heat levels, from Original (no spice) to Stark Hot.
