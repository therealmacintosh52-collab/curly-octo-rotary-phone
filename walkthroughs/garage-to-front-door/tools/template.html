The garage

Four spaces, one unbroken move through the house. Scroll to walk it.

01Garage

Where the walk begins

A full bay with the slab left open down the middle — room to work, room to park — and a door that goes straight into the house rather than around the outside.

  • Slab floor, runners to the door
  • Shelving and a bar overhead
  • Direct entry to the hall
02Hall

Through, and the house opens

The door gives onto a hall that runs the depth of the plan. Two rooms open off it, left and right, and past them there is a clean sightline all the way to the glass.

  • Plank floor, wall to wall
  • Rooms both sides
  • No turns, no steps
03Living area

Everything faces the glass

The hall releases into the main room, lit end to end by a two-panel slider. It is standing empty — which is the best way to see how much wall and floor there actually is.

  • Two-panel patio slider
  • Same floor as the hall
  • Open to the kitchen side
04Front entry

Out the front

The walk ends facing the front door — garage to street without a single turn, which is the part a plan drawing never quite tells you.

  • Six-panel door, lever and deadbolt
  • Floor runs to the threshold
  • Entry opens off the living area
