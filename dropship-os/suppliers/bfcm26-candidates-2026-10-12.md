# BFCM / Christmas 2026 — candidate shortlist with real landed costs

Pulled live from Zendrop on 2026-10-12 (China-direct "Zendrop Fulfillment",
supplier id 13). Landed = cheapest variant cost + Zendrop "regular" US
shipping. Transit is Zendrop's estimate to a US address. Market retail is
from a same-day WebSearch (Walmart / Amazon / Slickdeals snapshots, Oct
2026) — see sources at the bottom.

## The finding first

**Zendrop's China-direct shipping to the US runs $4.64–$30.08 per item.**
That, not product cost, is what breaks the §5 floor. For a product to
clear landed × 3 at a price customers pay, landed must be ≤ market ÷ 3;
with $7–17 shipping baked in, almost nothing under a $40 market price
qualifies. Transit is good (8 days quoted on most items) — better than
the 2–3 weeks assumed in the master doc — but the cost per parcel is
retail-parity for cheap goods.

**Result: 0 of 10 candidates clear 3× against market.** Three clear ~2×
(50% gross before fees). Combined with the US-stock finding (Amazon
relists at retail), Zendrop cannot support a 3× floor on anything we have
looked at. The pricing-floor decision is now binary: accept ~2× on a few
items, or move sourcing to a platform with cheaper small-parcel shipping.

**Bundling is the only lever that moves the math:** if Zendrop charges
shipping per order rather than per unit, a 2-pack of eye masks lands at
~$11.18 and sells at $29.95 (2.7×). This must be verified with a real
test order or Zendrop support before it is priced — the shipping-estimate
tool returns a single-unit figure only.

Budget per §4: $10–20/day each × 3–5 days = $90–300 total test spend.
Kill at ~$50 with no add-to-carts.

## What was rejected without a quote (from the 100-item trending list and keyword scans)

Pet toys, kitchen gadgets, fidgets, stickers, sponges, cleaning tools:
too cheap to carry $7–17 shipping; blood-pressure monitor (medical
device, regulatory exposure); heated jackets/vests (sizing returns);
anything with lithium batteries in heavy form factors (massage guns,
projector speakers) because of air-freight restrictions and the shipping
bands above.
