# Father's Day 2026 — Supplier Shopping List

Sourcing checklist for the five campaign products. A product activates
only when a real quote meets its **max landed cost** (product + shipping
+ fees ≤ ⅓ of retail) from a supplier that can deliver to US customers
**within ~5–7 days** (we're inside the June 21 window — US warehouses
only, no China-direct for this campaign).

**Where to look (accounts needed):**
- **Doba** — US warehouses, no MOQ; confirmed whiskey-stone suppliers
  (incl. ROCKS brand)
- **CJ Dropshipping** — filter: "US Warehouse"
- **AliExpress** — filter: "Ships from United States"
- **Zendrop / Spocket** — US-supplier directories, fast-ship focus
