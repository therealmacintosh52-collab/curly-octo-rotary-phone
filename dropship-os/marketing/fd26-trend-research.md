## Key takeaways

1. **Two of our five paper products were right** (thermometer, massager/
   multitool family) — but the catalog search terms were too specific.
   Search `handheld massager`, not "mini massage gun deep tissue 4 heads".
2. **Whiskey stones: drop.** Not surfaced in any 2026 trend source and
   the user confirmed it's not in CJ/Zendrop catalogs. Replace with the
   galaxy projector — the proven Q1 viral winner.
3. **The Bamboo Lazy Susan is back from the dead.** Home-org/rotating
   spice storage is one of 2026's strongest TikTok categories. The
   store's original product (currently drafted at $0.00) fits the #8
   trend exactly — worth sourcing properly and reviving for the
   post-FD evergreen catalog.
4. What sells on TikTok: visual transformation, problem-solving moment,
   or "wow" reveal in the first 2 seconds. Galaxy projector (reveal),
   thermometer (problem), massager (relief moment) all qualify.
5. Buyers are actively avoiding cliché dad gifts (ties, "World's Best
   Dad" mugs) — gadgets and "he'll actually use it" framing wins.
